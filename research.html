Research Work & Projects

I focus on computational astrophysics and cosmology, leveraging deep learning and scientific computing to explore galaxy formation, simulations, and data analysis.

Current Research

  • Deep Learning-based Super-Resolution for Cosmological Simulations
    Developing acceleration algorithms to enhance resolution in N-body galaxy formation simulations using deep neural networks, trained on Virgo Millennium and IllustrisTNG datasets.
  • Computational Analysis of Galaxy Evolution
    Using 2D projected galaxy images (mass density maps) to model and predict evolutionary patterns with AI.

Past Projects

  • Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for synthetic galaxy image generation (MIT research internship)
  • Astrophysics data pipelines integrating AI tools for Hindi language Question Answering systems
  • Development of astronomy outreach simulations and software demos

Publications & Presentations

  • Live webinar on Astronomical Simulations, Parbhani Astronomical Society (2020)
  • Demonstration of astronomical software at Rajashree Shahu College of Engineering, Pune
  • Multiple educational talks on Mars, Solar System, and space missions for schools and science clubs
