🔭 Research Experience

Deep Learning Based Acceleration for Galaxy Formation and Evolution Simulations

Supervisor: Dr. Aakash Patil

Dec 2023 – Present

Stanford University, California

I began my search for cosmological N-body simulations datasets for galaxy formation by studying IllustrisTNG50, IllustrisTNG100, IllustrisTNG300, and Millennium Simulations, which include large cosmological magnetohydrodynamical simulations. I investigated an existing deep learning super-resolution model applied to images and designed a super-resolution acceleration algorithm for cosmological N-body simulations. My model is trained on galaxy formation datasets from Virgo Millennium Simulation and Illustris Simulations. Currently, the model evaluation is underway.

Generation of Synthetic Galaxy Images with GAN

Supervisor: Prof. Mark Vogelsberger

Oct 2020 – Dec 2022

Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA

Our project designed chained neural networks using multiple GAN models to generate synthetic galaxy images, beginning with Progressive GAN. Using galaxy image datasets, we explored upsampling techniques and larger data inputs. To mitigate discriminator overfitting due to limited data, we investigated Adaptive Discriminator Augmentation mechanisms. We evaluate results with quantitative metrics like FID score, PSNR, and MS-SSIM.
