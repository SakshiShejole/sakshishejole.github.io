🎓 Academic Background

I began my academic journey with a Bachelor's in Engineering in Information Technology, where I developed a strong foundation in computing, programming, and software systems. My interest in scientific applications of technology led me to pursue a Master's degree in Scientific Computing. This interdisciplinary field allowed me to merge my love for science with computational techniques, ultimately guiding me toward research in astrophysics and artificial intelligence.

Throughout my studies, I have focused on applying machine learning, deep learning, and AI methodologies to scientific datasets, with a growing interest in cosmological simulations and data-driven astronomy. I’ve successfully taught subjects like Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Artificial Intelligence to undergraduate students, enhancing my understanding through academic engagement and instruction.

In 2024, I qualified the UGC NET exam in Computer Science & Applications, earning eligibility for doctoral research and reaffirming my commitment to academic excellence. I also participated in several domain-specific workshops and training programs that deepened my expertise in high-performance computing and research techniques used in astronomy and astrophysics.
