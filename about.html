🌟 About Me

I am an astronomy enthusiast, educator, and communicator with a strong background in space science outreach and research. My journey into the cosmos has been defined by an enduring love for the night sky and a mission to inspire others through education and immersive experiences. I've organized educational visits to prestigious facilities like the Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope, Nehru Planetarium, and Girawali Observatory. These opportunities enabled students and enthusiasts alike to connect directly with the universe through hands-on learning.

My passion led me to conduct sky-gazing trips for rural schoolchildren, combining astronomy with engaging activities like astro-games, real-time satellite tracking, and meteor shower observations. One of the milestones in my outreach career was being part of a core team that built a functional small-scale model of LIGO under the banner of Jyotirvidya Parisansthan, in collaboration with IUCAA.

Beyond field activities, I’ve delivered live webinars and on-site demonstrations of astronomical software to engineering students and budding astronomers. My talks have introduced diverse audiences to topics ranging from planetary science to space missions like the Mars Orbiter. Each interaction, whether with children in village schools or students in colleges, has been an opportunity to nurture scientific curiosity.

Alongside outreach, I’ve achieved several academic and professional milestones. I qualified the UGC NET (Computer Science & Applications) exam in December 2024, securing eligibility for doctoral studies. I also participated in a high-impact workshop on High Performance Computing in Astrophysics, organized by SKA-India and IIT Kharagpur, equipping me with advanced computational skills for space research.

As an event organizer and team coordinator, I’ve led initiatives like the "Aditya – Explore Our Sun" campaign, astroquizzes, mobile planetarium sessions, and curated the "Hubble and Beyond" exhibition. My contributions have been recognized with back-to-back "Best Event" awards in the Astronomy Club during my college years, and I proudly represented my batch as the Head Science Club Representative in my first year of engineering.

I hold a Basic Course certificate in Practical Astronomy from the Kesariwada Observatory, which laid the foundation for my exploration of observational techniques and stargazing tools. From then on, I've remained a devoted Astrogirl—sharing the sky, one star at a time.
