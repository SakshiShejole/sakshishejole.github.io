🌟 About Me

I'm deeply passionate about astronomy and space science, with years of experience in outreach, education, and hands-on astronomical activities. From organizing study tours to world-class observatories to inspiring rural students with sky-gazing events, I’ve committed my life to sharing the wonders of the cosmos.
